# Imagine Devs Digital

Create the complete Imagine Devs website using the 3 attached TXT files as the main source of truth.

The website must include a Home page and internal pages.

Home

Expertise

Case Studies

About

Contact

Internal pages:

- Expertise

- Case Studies

- Individual case study pages where specified

- About

- Contact

- Blog / Insights

- Individual blog article pages

CONTENT

Use:

1. Imagina_Devs_Home_Content.txt

2. Imagina_Devs_Expanded_Content.txt

3. Imagina_Devs_Visual_Style.txt

The TXT files define the content, structure, brand system and visual direction.

Do not add unnecessary sections or long copy. Keep the website simple, premium, modern and highly visual.

HOME

The Home should include:

- Hero with two rotating services:

  Staff Augmentation

  Salesforce Experts

- A single 3D particle system that transforms between the two hero states:

  Staff Augmentation → distributed / collaborative particle network

  Salesforce Experts → structured / connected technology network

- Client logo section immediately after the Hero.

- What We Do

- Industries & Expertise

- Selected Case Studies

- Insights / Blog preview

- Why Imagine Devs

- About preview

- Contact CTA

- Complete footer with navigation, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok.

INTERNAL PAGES

Build the internal pages using the content in Expanded Content.

Expertise:

Present Staff Augmentation, Salesforce Experts, Custom Software Development and Industries & Expertise.

Case Studies:

Create a case study listing page and individual pages for the documented cases, including Renuity and AgiOne.

About:

Create the complete About page.

Contact:

Create the contact page and form.

Blog / Insights:

Create the blog listing page and individual article pages using the proposed article topics and content direction in the files.

VISUAL DESIGN

Follow the Visual Style TXT exactly for:

- #062C37

- #92F252

- #00141B

- #EAFFE4

- Google Sans

- Phosphor Icons

- Glassmorphism

- Noisy gradients

- Photography treatment

- 3D particle animation

- Scroll animations

- Responsive behavior

- Buttons, cards, navigation and forms

Do not use long uppercase headlines or paragraphs.

Use uppercase mainly for short eyebrow / section labels.

Do not use pure black as the main text color.

DESIGN APPROACH

The website should feel:

Premium

Minimal

Modern

Technical

Editorial

Confident

Avoid generic SaaS aesthetics, excessive cards, excessive gradients, unnecessary animations and unnecessary copy.

Use generous whitespace and strong typography.

The visual references should inspire the design, but the Imagine Devs brand and attached TXT files take priority.

FUNCTIONALITY

Make all navigation and internal pages work correctly.

The Home navigation should link to the corresponding internal pages.

The website must be fully responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.

Use smooth but restrained scroll animations and transitions.

The Hero particle animation should be visually connected to the rotating service message rather than being a separate decorative animation.

IMPORTANT

Do not invent major services, claims, statistics or company information that are not supported by the attached files.

Where implementation details are not specified, make reasonable premium UI/UX decisions.
